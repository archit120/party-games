THE RULES OF THE TABLE

Trust is a dangerous thing.

5–10 players. Everyone uses their own device. Talk in person or on a voice call; keep your screen private.

Liberals win by enacting 5 Liberal policies or executing Hitler. Fascists win with 6 Fascist policies, or by electing Hitler Chancellor once 3 Fascist policies are on the board.

The President nominates an eligible Chancellor. Everyone votes secretly; a strict majority elects the government. A tie fails. The President draws 3 policies and discards 1; the Chancellor discards 1 and enacts the other. Neither legislator may communicate during this session.

The last elected Chancellor cannot be nominated again immediately. With more than 5 living players, the last elected President is also ineligible. After 3 failed elections or accepted vetoes, the top policy is enacted without a power and term limits clear. Only an enacted policy resets the election tracker.

Fascist policies grant powers based on the starting player count. Investigations reveal party membership, never Hitler’s identity; nobody can be investigated twice. Special elections return to the regular rotation afterward. Executed players cannot speak, vote, or hold office.

After 5 Fascist policies, the Chancellor may request a veto. The President must agree; otherwise the Chancellor must enact a policy. Accepted vetoes advance the election tracker.

Fascists know each other and Hitler. Hitler knows the Fascist only in 5–6 player games. You may lie about secret information.
