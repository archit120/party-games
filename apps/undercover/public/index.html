How to play

Most players receive the same word. Undercover players get a related word. Nobody with a word is told which team they are on. Optional Mr. White gets no word at all.

1 · DescribeTake turns giving a short clue. Do not say your secret word. Clues are public and stay in the room.

2 · DiscussCompare clues together or on a voice call. Any living player can open voting.

3 · VoteVote privately for another player. Once everybody votes, the most-voted player is eliminated and their role revealed.

4 · SurviveCivilians win by removing all impostors. Impostors win when they equal or outnumber the remaining civilians.

A tie triggers one runoff among tied players. A second tie eliminates nobody and starts a new clue round. An eliminated Mr. White gets one exact, case-insensitive guess at the civilian word; a correct guess wins immediately. Otherwise play continues. Eliminated players observe silently; their word remains private until the game ends.

An original Undercover-style party game · 3–12 players · Private rooms · No account needed
